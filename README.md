# Neon Swarm

Neon Swarm is a fast-paced, single-file arcade survival game. Pilot your ship, hold off the incoming swarm, collect gems to upgrade your weapon, and build score multipliers with chained kills.

## Play

Open [`Neon Swarm.html`](Neon%20Swarm.html) in a modern web browser and select **Start run**. No installation or build step is required. An internet connection is only needed to load the optional Syne font; the game uses a system-font fallback.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | WASD or arrow keys | Drag on the play area |u
| Dash | Space or Shift | Quick tap or use a second finger while moving |
| Pause or resume | P or Escape | Pause button |
| Toggle sound | M | Not available |

Your ship fires automatically at the nearest enemy. Collect the gems enemies drop to level up your weapon. Survive increasingly difficult waves, dash through danger, and chain kills before your combo expires to raise your score multiplier. You have three lives.

The game saves the top five scores in your browser's local storage.
